How to practice

Listen to the distance between the notes, then choose its name. Start with four intervals and add more in Practice settings when you feel comfortable. All notes use the same piano sound so you can focus on pitch.

A correct answer reveals both notes. Click the keyboard or highlighted string positions to play them, or choose Hear slowly. Wrong guesses let you try again; your score counts answers on the first try. Revealed answers count as missed.

Each view shows the exact pitches you heard. Guitar uses standard E–A–D–G–B–E tuning; violin and mandolin use G–D–A–E. On violin, dashed lines are semitone guides on an unfretted fingerboard.

Inspired by the interval exercises at TonedEar. A few minutes of regular practice goes a long way.
